## Claim under test — and the both-directions clause

The inclusion criterion (λ_j(1+βa²) > g_k) says reward-free objectives
fail to transfer because nothing amplifies reward-relevant support past
the boundary. FB (forward-backward; Touati–Ollivier line) is the one
reward-free family whose objective makes ANY reward's value readable
BY CONSTRUCTION (occupancy factorization M ≈ F(s,a,z)·B(s')). The
theory therefore predicts a FIRE — the escape. **Registered
consequence of a NO-ESCAPE outcome at the realized power: evidence
AGAINST the theory's ordering content, recorded as damage — never
instrument-blame** (instrument validity is carried by the gates, the
directional control, and the selfchecked harness; the verdict string
itself carries this sentence, fixed in the frozen reader).

## Honesty block

- Power: unknown zero-shot return dispersion; the fire bar (113.682) is
  ~1.4–1.6× the no-learning levels; MDE quoted inline on any null leg.
- action_repeat=1 (upstream default) vs dv3's repeat=2: returns compare
  at the episode-return level; the floor pins are adaptation-AUC-based
  no-learning levels (time-invariant for a non-learning policy) —
  DISCLOSED as a cross-protocol comparison; the fire bar is a level
  test, not a paired dv3 contrast.
- P-FB2's raw comparator is expected NEAR CEILING, not merely
  conservative (review M16a, computed pre-freeze on a SIBLING probeset
  as a well-posedness check, no FB quantity involved: raw 12-dim ridge
  AUROC ≈ 0.95 on finger_nz1_v1; dist_to_target alone ≈ 0.9999) — B
  must exceed ~0.95 with little headroom. A P-FB2 null is therefore
  weakly informative and the flagship weight is on P-FB1; registered
  estimand form: panel-conditional contrast, raw treated as fixed by
  construction (same panel, same folds; raw's own measurement error is
  not propagated — review M16b).
- Offline FB on curated 200-episode buffers is smaller-data than the
  ExORL settings the defaults were tuned for. **Training-health
  witness (review M17)**: every fit persists fb_metrics.json (final
  metrics + tail-median fb_loss, non-finite metrics refuse at fit
  time); the small-data/undertrained caveat is admissible against a
  NO-ESCAPE **only** if a fit's tail-median fb_loss falls outside
  **[-455.1604080200195, -4.551604080200195]** — FILLED by dated edit
  2026-08-16 (before any non-smoke fit) from the seed99 smoke's
  fb_metrics.json: fb_loss_tail_median = -45.51604080200195 (n_tail =
  100). Band form, chosen at this edit pre-outcome (the slot registered
  the anchor, not the width): a symmetric ×10 log-magnitude band around
  the smoke anchor, [10·m, m/10] for negative m. Rationale: the witness
  exists to catch scale anomalies (divergence, no-training) and must
  tolerate the registered 20000→500000-update extrapolation, so a
  generous order-of-magnitude band is the honest width — a fit more
  than 10× off the smoke's loss scale in either direction is a genuine
  training-health anomaly; anything inside is not an admissible
  undertraining caveat. Passed to the reader as
  `--health_band -455.1604080200195 -4.551604080200195` — otherwise it
  is not an admissible reading.
- **Escape-hatch ledger (review M17c)**: the ONLY admissible
  non-damage readings of a determinate NO-ESCAPE are (a) an enumerated
  reader gate refusal, (b) the random-floor control out of band, (c)
  the training-health witness out of its pinned band. The
  action_repeat caveat is RETIRED by the in-protocol random-floor
  control (a fixed policy's summed episode return is repeat-invariant).
  No caveat not in this list may be introduced after outcomes exist.
- Same-seed CPU refit determinism is selfchecked; GPU training
  determinism is NOT assumed (1-Aug rule); nothing pairs across
  invocations.
