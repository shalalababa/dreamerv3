## Class B — novel predictions (no existing data informs these; the theory's real test)

- **P-B1 (stamping, strong form).** Frozen-random-function stamping on
  reward-free buffers ⇒ approximately null transfer (band: within the
  apt null CIs). Alignment-graded stamps (dynamics-propagated or
  noisy-proxy labels) ⇒ transfer graded by alignment. **Refutation** =
  materially positive random-stamp effect; that outcome forces the
  subspace-expansion amendment (Part I §1.6) and kills the strong form.
  Either way the stamping experiment adjudicates
  "task-aligned-direction inclusion" vs "any stable scalar supervision."
- **P-B2 (value wake-up; conditional on wiring audit).** An arm whose
  value targets contain out-of-subspace TRUE-reward signal (Monte-Carlo
  or oracle returns from environment rewards) transfers rgo-like (> sgb);
  an arm with model-internal (imagination-bootstrap) targets ≡ sgb.
  Conditionality: whether historical vgo was case (a) imagination-only
  or case (b) replay-grounded must be determined from saved configs
  BEFORE this prediction is tested; if vgo was case (b), the theory
  instead predicts attenuated-but-nonzero vgo signal recoverable at
  longer fit budgets, and P-B2 shifts to that contrast.
- **P-B3 (label noise channel).** Label noise shifts the *apparent*
  occupancy threshold right at fixed fit steps, and extending fit steps
  substantially undoes the shift (population threshold is noise-free;
  noise acts through finite-time/finite-sample channels only).
- **P-B4 (count vs fraction).** At fixed rewarded-frame fraction,
  scaling total buffer size does not move the threshold once above the
  estimation floor (membership competition runs on fractions).
- **P-B5 (scaling dissociation).** With model size: (i) apt−task gap in
  asymptotic adaptation performance shrinks; (ii) the gap in
  fixed-budget AUC100k shrinks strictly less (may persist);
  (iii) directional secondary: apt AUC100k may be non-monotone in size
  (readout dilution). Testable with the pilot's existing metrics.
- **P-B6 (support boundary).** Occupancy cells below the size1m
  threshold remain null at larger model sizes (capacity does not
  substitute for absent/insufficient support).
- **P-B7 (threshold moves left with training).** The apparent occupancy
  threshold decreases with fit steps; the cell nearest threshold at the
  standard budget flips with an extended budget.
